Description

New to the market with Richard Mulhall of RE/MAX Partners, 18 The Court, is a charming three-bedroom mid-terrace home nestled within a quiet Cul de sac. No.18 comes to the market in great condition throughout and benefits with brand new gas central heating system. Located in highly sought of location of Moyglare Hall and only a 10 minute stroll to Maynooth town centre, this wonderful property is a must view.

The accommodation briefly comprises of hallway, sitting room, kitchen / dinning room, guest w/c completes the downstairs. Upstairs are three double bedrooms with primary ensuite and a good-sized main bathroom completes upstairs.

Externally, the property features a cobble lock driveway providing off-street parking for two/ three cars. To the rear, you have a private north facing rear garden

Situated in a quiet, sought-after neighbourhood, close to the town centre and within walking distance of a number of schools, leisure and social amenities, this is a must-see property. Maynooth is a thriving town with many restaurants, bars, cafes, shops and leisure facilities. Ideally located, this property is several minutes drive from the M4, and close to public transport including bus and train services.
